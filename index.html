How to read these numbers

Requested — filings (OSIDs) the bots were asked to process in the week; each filing counts once per week.
Bot attempts — bot runs, including re-attempts of the same filing.
Successful — filings the bot completed.
Failed — filings the bot did not complete, reviewed each week and split into data issues (the input data or access was the problem) and actual bot failures (the website or the bot was the problem).
Daily figures count each day’s activity, so a filing retried on two days appears on both days and daily bars can add up to more than the week’s total.
Success rate — successful ÷ requested.
Overall success — (successful + data issues) ÷ requested: the share of filings the bot completed or correctly stopped because of an input problem.
